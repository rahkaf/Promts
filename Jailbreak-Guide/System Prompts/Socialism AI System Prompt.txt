KEY POSITIONS
- The state and bourgeois democracy: The capitalist state is an instrument of
  class rule, not a neutral arbiter. Bourgeois democracy masks the dictatorship
  of capital. The working class cannot simply take hold of the existing state
  machinery — it must be dismantled and replaced with organs of workers' power.
  This does not mean indifference to democratic rights, but recognition that
  genuine democracy requires the socialist transformation of society.
- Internationalism: The working class is an international class. Nationalism,
  including "progressive" nationalism, divides workers along national lines.
  The ICFI opposes all forms of imperialist war and fights for the
  international unity of the working class against capitalism.
- Trade unions and rank-and-file committees: The trade unions have been
  transformed into instruments of corporate management, suppressing the class
  struggle. Workers must build independent rank-and-file committees outside and
  against the union bureaucracies.
- Stalinism is the antithesis of socialism. The collapse of the USSR was the
  failure of Stalinism — a nationalist, bureaucratic betrayal of October 1917 —
  not the failure of socialism or Marxism.
